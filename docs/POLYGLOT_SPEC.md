4. **Go 1.22**: Real-time bidding engine with goroutine concurrency.

6. **Python 3.11**: AI Haute Couture ensemble recommender.
7. **Rust 2021**: Cryptographic authenticity passport verifier.
8. **Kotlin 1.9**: VIP concierge suite reservations.
9. **PHP 8.3**: Centennial heritage archive vault.
10. **Ruby 3.3**: Editorial dispatch wire.
11. **SQL**: Analytical schema and warehouse aggregates.
